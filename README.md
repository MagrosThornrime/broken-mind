# Broken Mind
https://piotr135.itch.io/broken-mind

## Introduction
Your whole world is collapsing. It's a matter of time when you will fall into the abyss. The only thing you can do is to take as many void monsters with you as possible.
Push them into the darkness while avoiding their magic. Also, stay in the world as long as you can!

## Game mechanics
You have two abilities. First one is the bomb. You can use it to destroy tiles in fron of you. It will help you fighting with enemies. But it will also make it harder for you to survive.
You can also regenerate the map. Use it wisely.

If you don't have enough lifes, you can collect them from the map - they spawn randomly. Always have some spare health - you don't know when a tile will break right under you.

Each pushed enemy will give you 1 point. After cleaning the entire map, the next wave will spawn.

## Controls
- Move up: "W" or "Arrow up"
- Move down: "S" or "Arrow down"
- Move left: "A" or "Arrow left"
- Move right: "D" or "Arrow right"
- Bomb: "G"
- Regenerate the world: "Space"
